# Business Questions & Analytical Framework

## Executive
- Revenue, completed orders, active customers and AOV
- Monthly revenue trend and growth

## Customer
- Customer lifetime/recent value proxy
- High-value segmentation
- Revenue concentration
- Inactive or low-engagement customers
- Missing customer contact information

## Employee
- Revenue by employee
- Department ranking
- Employee versus department average
- Revenue contribution by department

## Product
- Product revenue ranking
- Category contribution
- Units sold
- Gross-to-net discount impact

## Management Recommendations Framework

For every result, ask:

1. **What happened?** — KPI/result
2. **Why did it happen?** — segment/product/employee driver
3. **Why does it matter?** — financial or operational impact
4. **What should management do?** — concrete action

This keeps the project focused on business decisions rather than SQL syntax alone.
